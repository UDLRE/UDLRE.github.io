UDLRE: Restoration for Ultra-Dark Image with Differentiable Implict Parameter Prediction and Frequency Enhancement

Yanming Hui, Fanhua Shang*, Hongying Liu*
Tianjin University
Code Data

Abstract

While image super-resolution methods have made significant progress under normal lighting conditions, their performance severely degrades in ultra-dark environments. To address this, we propose the Ultra-Dark and Low Resolution image Enhancement network (UDLRE), an integrated and lightweight framework. Specifically, we design a Differentiable Implicit Parameter Prediction (DIPP) module. Operating as a plug and play component, DIPP achieves spatially adaptive brightness enhancement through pixel-wise implicit features and dynamic coefficients. To tackle the severe loss of details, we introduce a Frequency space Super-Resolution Reconstruction (FSRR) module. It employs the fast fourier transform to decouple images into high and low frequency components, applying dedicated enhancement blocks to independently reconstruct structural details. Furthermore, to bridge the semantic gap between representations, we present a Dual Domain Joint Attention (DDJA) mechanism that links spatial channel features with frequency spectral features. This establishes a smooth bidirectional information flow, maximizing feature utilization efficiency. Extensive experiments demonstrate that UDLRE outperforms state of the art algorithms by 8.7% in PSNR and 6.7% in SSIM, while requiring approximately 52 times fewer parameters.
